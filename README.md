## Project Overview

Developed a comprehensive Social Media Marketing (SMM) strategy for **Aero Fit**, a fitness footwear brand, with a focus on increasing customer and revenue market share through digital channels.

The project covered competitor analysis, social media strategy, content planning, paid advertising, audience targeting, conversion tracking, and platform management.

## Objectives

* Strengthen Aero Fit's presence across major social media platforms.
* Increase customer and revenue market share through digital marketing.
* Reach relevant B2C and B2B audiences.
* Improve engagement and support conversions through paid and organic social media activities.

## Competitor Analysis

Analyzed competitors including:

* Nidas
* Dike
* Buma
* Teebok

The analysis was used to identify positioning opportunities and develop social media strategies for Aero Fit.

## Social Media Strategy

Developed a monthly content calendar covering:

* Platform-specific content
* Captions
* Posting schedules
* Promotional content
* Engagement-focused content

## Meta Marketing

* Allocated **45% of the social media budget to Meta platforms** based on the project's reported sales impact.
* Created an Aero Fit Facebook Business Page.
* Published a flash-sale campaign with relevant hashtags.
* Planned Meta Ads targeting users aged **21–40** interested in fitness, gyms, and sports across India.

## LinkedIn & YouTube Strategy

### LinkedIn

Created a LinkedIn Business Page targeting:

* Gym owners
* Corporate audiences
* B2B prospects

### YouTube

Created an Aero Fit YouTube channel with:

* Professional channel description
* Brand logo
* Website link

## Meta Pixel & Conversion Tracking

Installed Meta Pixel on WordPress and configured:

* PageView
* AddToCart
* Purchase

These events were planned to support conversion tracking and campaign optimization.

## Content Scheduling

Used **Facebook Content Library** to schedule social media content and streamline publishing and campaign management.
